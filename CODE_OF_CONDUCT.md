# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of identity,
background, experience, or ability. Research disagreement is welcome when it is specific,
evidence-based, and directed at the work rather than the person.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, precise, and constructive.
- Respect privacy, licensing, consent, and research-data restrictions.
- Credit contributors and sources accurately.
- Accept correction and make room for participants with different experience levels.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publication of private
or restricted data are unacceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when this code is violated. Report conduct concerns privately through the
repository's GitHub security-advisory reporting channel. If that channel is unavailable, open a
minimal issue asking the maintainer for a private contact route; do not include confidential
details in the issue. Maintainers will handle reports as privately and promptly as practical,
without promising outcomes that cannot be guaranteed.
